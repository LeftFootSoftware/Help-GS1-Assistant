## Main Page



 Manage and generate official GS1 GTINs and UPCs directly within Shopify. This tool helps you stay retail-compliant by connecting with GS1 US to ensure your barcodes are verifiable and trusted by major retailers like Amazon and Walmart. Whether you already have a GS1 Company Prefix or need to get one, this app guides you through the process quickly and accurately.
