## GS1 Assistant pricing: plans, usage charges, cancelling and refunds

GS1 Assistant (UPC EAN GTIN) is a Shopify app by Left Foot Software, not a GS1 organization. It has three plans: **Essential** at $0 a month, **Growth** at $4.99 a month, and **Automation** at $29.99 a month or $299.99 a year. Essential and Growth also have usage charges for the GTINs you assign each month. All charges appear on your Shopify bill, and GS1 Assistant takes no commission or share of your sales.

Prices checked: 21 November 2026. The [GS1 Assistant listing in the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/gs1-assistant) always shows the current prices.

### What does each GS1 Assistant plan cost?

All prices are in US dollars. Monthly prices and usage charges are billed every 30 days; the yearly price is billed once a year.

| Plan | Monthly price | Yearly price | Prefix size | Usage charges | Key features | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | $0 | None | Up to 100 GTINs | Yes | Assign GTIN, UPC and EAN to your products. Find missing and misformatted GTINs. Import files for 10 GS1 organizations. | Email and Zoom setup and onboarding |
| **Growth** | $4.99 | None | Up to 1,000 GTINs | Yes | Everything in Essential, plus API Synchronization (where supported). | Email and Zoom setup and onboarding |
| **Automation** | $29.99 | $299.99 (the listing says "save 17%") | Any GS1 company prefix | None shown | Everything in Growth, plus Auto Assign GTINs to new products and Auto Sync where your GS1 organization has an API. | Priority email and Zoom |

If your Shopify bill is in a local currency, Shopify converts app charges from US dollars at a rate that includes a 1.5% currency exchange fee.

### How do usage charges work?

On **Essential** and **Growth**, you pay for the GTINs you assign each month, on top of the plan price. The first GTIN you assign each month is free on both plans. The two plans count the rest in different ways.

**Essential: your monthly total sets one charge.** The number of GTINs you assign in the month decides the charge for all of them:

| GTINs you assign in a month | Usage charge for the month |
|---|---|
| 1 | $0 |
| 2 to 10 | $4.99 |
| 11 or more | $9.99 |

**Growth: you pay a price for each GTIN, by tier.** Each tier's price applies only to the GTINs inside that tier:

| GTINs you assign in a month | Price for each GTIN in this tier |
|---|---|
| 1st | $0 |
| 2nd to 10th | $1.00 |
| 11th to 100th | $0.50 |
| 101st to 1,000th | $0.05 |
| 1,001st and more | $0.01 |

**Automation:** the listing shows no usage charges. You pay $29.99 a month or $299.99 a year.

Shopify bills app usage charges on a 30-day billing cycle for each app.

### Examples: what you pay in one month

| Plan and GTINs you assign | Plan price | Usage charge | Total for the month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential, 1 GTIN | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Essential, 5 GTINs | $0 | $4.99 | $4.99 |
| Essential, 20 GTINs | $0 | $9.99 | $9.99 |
| Growth, 1 GTIN | $4.99 | $0 | $4.99 |
| Growth, 10 GTINs | $4.99 | $9.00 (1 free + 9 × $1.00) | $13.99 |
| Growth, 50 GTINs | $4.99 | $29.00 (1 free + 9 × $1.00 + 40 × $0.50) | $33.99 |

At 50 GTINs a month, Growth costs more than Automation's $29.99 monthly price.

### Is there a free plan, and what happens when I go past it?

Yes. **Essential** costs $0 a month. You can keep assigning GTINs after your first free one; usage charges apply from the second GTIN in the month.

The plan you need depends on the size of your GS1 company prefix:

- **Up to 100 GTINs:** Essential works.
- **More than 100 GTINs:** you need **Growth**.
- **More than 1,000 GTINs:** you need **Automation**.

The old limit of 20 GTINs belonged to the retired Free plan. It does not apply to Essential.

### Is there a free trial?

There is no free trial. The Essential plan is $0 a month, so you can try GS1 Assistant on it.

### Which plan fits you?

Choose a plan by the size of your GS1 company prefix, and by whether you want new products handled automatically.

- **Your prefix holds more than 100 GTINs:** you need **Growth** at least. Over 1,000 GTINs, you need **Automation**. Check your prefix size under **Settings** > **Prefixes** (**Size**).
- **Your prefix is from GS1 Germany, Netherlands, Sweden or Brazil:** as of November 2026, these GS1 organizations have an API that GS1 Assistant connects to. On **Growth** (API Synchronization) and **Automation** (Auto Sync), GS1 Assistant sends each GTIN to GS1 when you assign it.
- **You add new products often:** **Automation** gives each new variant a GTIN with Auto Assign, which runs through the **Assign GTIN to Variant** action in Shopify Flow. See [Using Shopify Flow to Assign GTINs to New Products](https://gs1assistant.crisp.help/en/article/using-shopify-flow-to-assign-gtins-to-new-products-1qol6ru/).
- **You assign many GTINs each month on Growth:** a month with more than 42 GTINs costs more than Automation's $29.99 monthly price (42 GTINs on Growth cost exactly $29.99). On Essential, usage charges stop at $9.99 a month, however many GTINs you assign.

### Do I still pay GS1?

Yes. GS1 Assistant does not sell GTINs or include them in any plan. It assigns GTINs from a GS1 company prefix that you license from a GS1 organization. You pay your GS1 organization for the prefix and the membership, separately from GS1 Assistant. GS1 Assistant does not manage your GS1 renewals or billing.

GS1 Assistant works only with a GS1 company prefix. It does not work with GTINs bought from a barcode reseller.

**In the US:** if you have no prefix yet, you can license one from GS1 US inside GS1 Assistant. Left Foot Software is a GS1 US Channel Partner. You choose a prefix package in that window. The package price is not part of your GS1 Assistant plan.

### How do I check or change my plan?

1. Open **GS1 Assistant**.
2. Go to the **Dashboard**. Your plan shows under **Plan Type**.
3. To change it, click **Change Plan** and choose a plan.
4. Approve the new charge when Shopify asks.

The footer at the bottom of each GS1 Assistant page also shows your plan, for example "Plan: Pro". Stores that subscribed earlier can see an older plan name.

When you move to a more expensive plan, Shopify charges the difference for the days left in the billing cycle. When you move to a cheaper plan, Shopify offers an app credit for the days left. You can use the credit toward future app charges on Shopify.

### How do I cancel?

To stop the monthly plan price, change to **Essential** with **Change Plan**. Essential costs $0 a month, but usage charges still apply when you assign GTINs.

To stop all GS1 Assistant charges, uninstall the app:

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > **Apps**.
2. Next to **GS1 Assistant**, click the **⋯** menu, then **Uninstall**.
3. Optional: choose a reason for uninstalling.
4. Click **Uninstall**.

Uninstalling stops future billing cycles. A charge that Shopify has already generated for your next bill can still appear on it. If you want to ask for a refund, contact us before you uninstall.

Before you uninstall, download any export files you need. Shopify notes that some app data cannot be restored after you uninstall. If you install GS1 Assistant again later, review your earlier assignments before you assign new GTINs. See [How to Avoid Duplicate GTIN Assignments](https://gs1assistant.crisp.help/en/article/how-to-avoid-duplicate-gtin-assignments-6wuc5a/).

### Can I get a refund?

Shopify bills app charges, but refund decisions for GS1 Assistant charges are ours. To ask for a refund, contact us:

- In GS1 Assistant, click **Help** > **Chat with us**.
- Or, in your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > **Apps**, click **GS1 Assistant**, then click **Get support**.

Include the charge amount and date from your Shopify bill, and the reason for your request. We review each request.

### Older plans (no longer offered)

These plans no longer appear on the listing:

- **Until 28 September 2026:** Free (20 GTINs), Basic at $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year (500 GTINs), and Pro at $11.99 a month or $110 a year (unlimited GTINs).
- **Briefly from 29 September 2026 (gone by 2 October 2026):** Pro at $49.99 a month or $499.99 a year.

If your app still shows an older plan name, your price is the one on your Shopify bill. To move to a current plan, use **Change Plan**.
