## Capabilities by Region

> **Important:** GS1 Assistant works with prefixes from **all** GS1 Member Organizations worldwide. The tables below only indicate where optional **direct synchronization** with a Member Organization's internal repository is available. If a region is listed under *Other Regions*, you can still manage prefixes and GTINs fully in GS1 Assistant; you'll just use **manual import/export** or the MO's portal instead of a direct sync.

> **Optional:** You can choose **not** to use a Member Organization's repository at all. Register GTINs in an MO repository only if:
>
> - The MO repository feeds **regional** retailer/marketplace catalogs, or
> - You need your products listed in **Verified by GS1**.

> **Legend:** ✅ • 🟡 • ❌ • ❓ = info pending

> **Membership note:** GS1 global guidance historically suggests "one MO per company," but in practice many MOs accept non-local members. Always verify eligibility with the MO.
