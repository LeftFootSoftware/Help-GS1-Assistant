## Set Your GS1 Defaults

Fill in your GS1 Defaults once, so the product details GS1 needs are filled in for you each time you download an import file.

### What GS1 Defaults do

GS1 Defaults hold the details most of your products share, such as your brand, product category and language. When you open the **Download** window for an import file, GS1 Assistant fills in those fields from your defaults. You can still change them in the window before you click **Ok**.

Without defaults, the file can carry the wrong values. For example, an empty brand puts your store name in the file, and the GS1 USA language starts at **aa**, which leaves the language columns empty.

### Fill in your GS1 Defaults

1. In the GS1 Assistant menu, click **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **GS1 Defaults**.
3. Fill in the fields that apply to most of your products:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Target Market** | The country where your product is sold |
| **Category** | Your product's GPC brick. Type a word to search, or click **GPC Browser** |
| **Brand** | Your brand name, as it appears on the product |
| **Language** | The language of your product text in the target market, for example English (en) |
| **Net quantity** | The weight, volume or number of units in the product |
| **Unit of measure** | The unit of the net quantity |
| **Consumer Unit** | Whether a shopper can buy the product on its own |
| **Packaging Level** | The packaging level of the product, for example **Each** |
| **Product Language** | The language of the product. Leave it empty if you are not sure |
| **NCM** | Brazil only: the 8-digit Mercosur code, numbers only |

4. Click **Save** in the bar at the top of the page.

### Tips

- **Category needs a brick.** GS1 portals reject a higher GPC level. Brick codes start with 1, for example 10001352 (Shirts/Blouses/Polo Shirts/T-shirts). Search by a simple word such as "shirt" or "bag".
- **Defaults are a starting point.** If a product differs, for example a different brand, change the field in the **Download** window for that file.
