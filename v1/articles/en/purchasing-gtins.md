## Purchasing GTINs Through the App



**Getting Started**

If you need a company prefix to get started, GS1 Assistant can help you choose the right GS1 Member Organization for your business. Whether you need UPCs or EANs, the app provides recommendations based on your location and barcode type needs.

**Recommendations**

- **For U.S.-based businesses:** If you're a U.S.-based business needing UPC barcodes, we recommend **GS1 US**, the official source for UPCs recognized by Amazon, Walmart, Target, and other major U.S. retailers.

- **For European sellers:** For European sellers or those needing EANs, **GS1 Germany** is a cost-effective and reliable option. GS1 Germany supports Verified by GS1 and is widely accepted by European retailers and marketplaces. It also offers seamless integration with GS1 Assistant through their API, making it easy to sync and manage your GTINs directly from your Shopify store.

**Purchasing from GS1 US**

As a GS1 US Channel Partner, GS1 Assistant allows you to purchase official GS1 GTINs and UPCs directly through the app. You can purchase up to **1,000 GTINs at a time** from GS1 US directly through the app.

The app presents various pricing tiers for purchasing GTINs:

- **1-10 GTINs:** $250.00
- **1-100 GTINs:** $750.00
- **1-1,000 GTINs:** $2,500.00
- **1-10,000 GTINs:** $6,500.00
- **1-100,000 GTINs:** $10,500.00

**Note:** While packages up to 100,000 GTINs are available, you can purchase up to 1,000 GTINs at a time directly through the app. For larger quantities, you may need to visit the GS1 US website directly.

**Purchasing Process**

1. Select the package that best suits your needs from the available options.
2. Fill in your contact information, including first name, last name, and phone number (all required fields are marked with *).
3. Complete the payment process to finalize your purchase.

For information about GS1 Germany pricing, you can access their pricing page through the app.
