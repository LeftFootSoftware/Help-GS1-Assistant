## License a GS1 US Company Prefix in GS1 Assistant

If you sell in the US and have no GS1 company prefix yet, license one from GS1 US inside GS1 Assistant. Left Foot Software, the maker of GS1 Assistant, is a GS1 US Channel Partner, so you can choose a package without leaving the app.

### Before you start

- **This is for GS1 US only.** For another country, contact the GS1 organization of the country where your business is registered. In the window, **Find My GS1 Member Organization** helps you find it.
- **Already have a prefix?** You do not need a new one. Add it under **Settings** > **Prefixes**. See [Add and Manage Your Company Prefix](https://gs1assistant.crisp.help/en/article/adding-and-managing-your-company-prefix-6cxy7p/).
- **Decide how many GTINs you need.** You need one GTIN for each variant, for example each size and color of a shirt. Leave room for products you plan to add.

### License a prefix

1. In the GS1 Assistant menu, click **Settings**.
2. In the **Prefixes** card, click the arrow next to **Add**.
3. Click **Get a New Prefix**. The **License Company Prefix** window opens.
4. Select a package. Each package shows how many GTINs it holds and its price.
5. Click **Continue**.

![screenshot:The License Company Prefix window. The five packages (4), from 1-10 for $250.00 to 1-100,000 for $10,500.00, and the Continue button (5) are outlined. Find My GS1 Member Organization sits next to Continue.](LicensePrefixWindow.png)

6. Follow the steps that open to complete your purchase.

The prices above are as shown in the app in November 2026. GS1 US sets the terms of the licence, including any yearly fee. Check them before you pay.

### After you buy

- **Your prefix is registered to your business.** GS1 US licenses it in your company's name, and the GTINs show under that name in Verified by GS1.
- **GS1 US invites you to Data Hub,** its online portal, where you add your product details.
- **Check your prefix in GS1 Assistant.** Open **Settings** > **Prefixes** and look for your new prefix. If it is not there, add it with **Add Existing Prefix**. If your GS1 US certificate shows a U.P.C. Company Prefix, add a 0 to the front, for example 12345 becomes 012345.

Then assign GTINs to your variants. See [Assign GTINs to Many Variants at Once](https://gs1assistant.crisp.help/en/article/bulk-gtin-assignment-assign-barcodes-to-your-entire-catalogue-at-once-1vrtodi/).

### Tips

- **Already have a GS1 US account?** Buying a second prefix here can open a separate GS1 US account. To combine the two, ask GS1 US about a prefix transfer.
- **Choose a package with room to grow.** Larger packages cost less per GTIN: 1-10 is $25.00 per GTIN, 1-100 is $7.50.
