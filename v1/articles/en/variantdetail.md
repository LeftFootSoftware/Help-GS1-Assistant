## Variant Detail



**GTIN Status**

&nbsp;&nbsp;Shows the current GTIN, its validation status (e.g., OK), company prefix, and the registered company details. Includes the option to unassign the GTIN.

**Verified by GS1 Card**

&nbsp;&nbsp;Confirms that the product is officially verified in the GS1 registry. Displays verified details like description, brand name, category, net content, and verified image.

**GTIN Manager**

&nbsp;&nbsp;Allows users to view and edit product data synced with the GS1 catalog. This includes categorization, language, market, net quantity, packaging level, etc. It also allows syncing or updating this info to GS1.

**GTIN Log History**

&nbsp;&nbsp;A chronological log showing all actions and sync events related to the GTIN—such as assignments, barcode updates, GS1 syncs, and clear operations.
